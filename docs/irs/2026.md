# 2026

## Workplace Contribution Limits

### 403b Employee Elective Deferral Limit

**Note**: this limit is shared with any other 403b/401k employee elective deferrals for the year (e.g. a [SUSORP](../usf/susorp.md) Voluntary contribution paired with a [Voluntary 403b](../usf/vol-403b) contribution or a 401k contribution at another job).

- Standard: $24,500
- Catch-up (age 50-59 or 64+): $8,000 additional contribution
- Catch-up (age 60-63): $11,250 additional contribution

### 457b Employee Elective Deferral Limit

Since the 457b (deferred compensation) is treated by the IRS as an entirely different bucket.
Its essentially a second 401k/403b.

- Standard: $24,500
- Catch-up (age 50-59 or 64+): $8,000 additional contribution
- Catch-up (age 60-63): $11,250 additional contribution

### Notes

- If you are age 50 or older and earned $150,000 or more in the prior year (Box-3 on your 2025 W-2), any catch-up contributions to your workplace retirement plan must be made as a Roth contribution using after-tax dollars (IRS will adjust this amount each year for inflation).

- For catch-up contributions, you are eligible if you will be the required age by the last day of the year. E.g.: If you are 49, but will be 50 on December 31, 2026, you may make the 50+ catch-up contribution for 2026 since you will be 50 by the end of 2026).
