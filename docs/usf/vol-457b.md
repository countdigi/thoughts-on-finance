# Voluntary 457b

The [457 Florida Deferred Compensation Plan](https://www.usf.edu/hr/benefits/retirement/voluntary-retirement.aspx)
is an additional plan essentially acting as a second 401k with a separate IRS limit.

It also has special IRS rules allowing you to take distributions before 59.5 years of age without early withdrawal penalties.

Congress designed 457(b) plans primarily for state and local government workers (such as police officers, firefighters, and state employees) who often have pension structures that allow or require them to retire earlier than the standard private-sector retirement age. Exempting the plan from the 10% penalty allows these public servants to seamlessly use their deferred compensation as bridge income between retirement and when their regular pensions or Social Security benefits kick in.
