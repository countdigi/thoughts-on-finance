# Voluntary 403b

The [USF 403b Savings Plan](https://www.usf.edu/hr/benefits/retirement/voluntary-retirement.aspx) is
a tax-savings retirement plan similar in structure to a 401k.

It can be opened in addition to an FRS Pension, [FRS Investment](../usf/frs-inv), or [SUSORP](../usf/susorp) plan.

**Note**:

- Employee contributions from the [SUSORP](../usf/susorp) plan count towards the IRS 403b limit so you must stay under this limiit for both plans (details at [SUSORP](../usf/susorp)).
- The FRS Pension and [FRS Investment](../usf-frs-inv) plans are classified as a 401a so this allows you to contribute up to the full IRS 403b limit.
